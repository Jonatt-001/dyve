ABOUT

The Intelligence Unit

The DYVE TECH INTELLIGENCE UNIT is dedicated to analyzing the most critical shifts in the global technology landscape. From Agentic AI to quantum computing, we provide actionable insights for enterprise and tech leaders.

Our research focuses on how autonomous agents are reshaping workflows, the rapid evolution of deepfake technology, and the emergence of new cybersecurity challenges in 2026 and beyond.

We also track geopolitical tech strategies, including Europe’s IRIS² satellite internet initiative and the ongoing debates over data privacy and user data access.

Our mission is to cut through the noise, delivering high-value intelligence on open-source platforms, vibe-coding, and the future of global connectivity.

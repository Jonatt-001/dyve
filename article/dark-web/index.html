DOMAIN BRIEF

What actually exists beyond the surface web?

The dark web is a portion of the internet that is intentionally hidden from conventional indexing systems and typically accessed through specialized networks such as Tor.

Its privacy architecture has legitimate uses, including anonymous communication and censorship resistance. The same architecture, however, can also support criminal marketplaces, stolen-data distribution, access trading and other underground activity.

For cybersecurity teams, the important question is not simply whether something exists on the dark web. The intelligence value comes from determining whether an observation is credible, whether it relates to a real organization or infrastructure, and whether it represents an actionable threat.

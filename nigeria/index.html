09 / Frequently Asked Questions

Nigeria cybersecurity, explained.

What is Nigeria Cyber Threat Intelligence?
Nigeria Cyber Threat Intelligence is the collection, analysis and contextualization of cybersecurity threats, vulnerabilities, malware, phishing, ransomware, incidents and other security signals relevant to Nigeria.
What cybersecurity threats are affecting Nigeria?
The Nigerian threat landscape includes phishing, ransomware, business email compromise, malware, exposed services, credential theft, data breaches, denial-of-service activity and exploitation of vulnerable internet-facing systems.
Where does Dyve get its cybersecurity intelligence?
Dyve can aggregate public cybersecurity advisories, vulnerability databases, threat intelligence feeds, vendor disclosures and security research. Individual intelligence records should identify the relevant original source.
Is this a live Nigerian cyber attack monitor?
This initial version is a public intelligence hub rather than a proprietary real-time sensor network. The platform is designed to progressively incorporate structured threat feeds and automated intelligence processing as the Dyve Nigeria intelligence system develops.
